# Quantity balance comparisons (developer-only)

Use `python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_scale_comparison.json > balance.json` to compare explicitly authored groups of installed Facilities and changes in their scale. This runs **no canonical day, Player Command, auto-procurement or gameplay optimizer**. It reads the currently registered Content Definitions and outputs nominal quantities, not claims of executable progress.

A plan has `initial_stock` (node/resource/amount_t), `initial_facilities` (node/facility/units plus optional process/extraction/duty), and a sequence of `phases` (name, days, complete set of facilities in that phase). If the Facility count rises, the difference is counted as a new construction investment at phase start, with the construction-work demand reported independently; construction does **not** wait for the capacity to finish. Removing Facilities is rejected because no retirement, salvage and dismantling model is applied. Initial Facility counts are owned historical assets and do not incur a new construction cost. All subsequent phase counts are explicit investment assumptions, not an automatic plan.

An asset can select one compatible `process` ID, with `duty` in `[0, 1]`. Its daily Process inputs/outputs are multiplied by the registered Facility throughput, units and duty. A selected `extraction` method can supply a nominal upper bound; `extraction_yield_fraction` in `[0, 1]` is an **explicit scenario assumption** (default zero), not a derived geology/Knowledge result. Solar generators require `solar_yield` by node, the authored ratio of actual solar flux times availability to their reference flux, which may exceed 1. Without it the scenario fails instead of inventing power.

Phases may declare `external_rates` (node/resource/`net_t_per_day`, signed), `transfers` (origin/destination/resource/`t_per_day`), `vehicle_builds` (node/vehicle/units), and `research_stages` (node/research/stage ID). These are assumed quantities, not Market orders, Fleet trips or completed Research Stages. Transfers conserve named Resource mass across sites but do **not** include in-transit latency, routing, fuel, cargo queues, exact handling settlement or Fleet availability. Vehicle and Research rows consume their Definition costs, and required work/RP are reported, but progression is not scheduled.

`resources` keeps production, Process inputs, Maintenance, Construction, Vehicle, Research material, external transfers and shortages in distinct columns **per Resource and node**. Deficits are signed notional stock; negative stock is *not* legal Game State and its propagation across phases is only unmet planned balance. Power, construction, cargo handling and special physical storage are **nominal** quantity comparisons. The model does not reproduce Technology/Site/Knowledge eligibility, Activity Priority, usable versus physical Storage, actual Market funds, construction times, extraction saturation, Population, or Research Point production. These omissions prevent treating any result as a gameplay feasibility verdict.

An input with top-level `cases` (each `{name, plan}`) also returns `differences`, computed against the first case for matching phase names and durations. Values are separated by physical dimension. Do not aggregate unlike Resources or compare unmatched time windows as equivalent. Independent `evaluation_tests/test_analysis_balance.py` verifies the arithmetic and conservation of these projections; live Domain/Game contracts remain in `tests/`.
