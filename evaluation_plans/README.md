# Quantity balance comparisons (developer-only)

Use `python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_scale_comparison.json --summary` for a compact overview, then `python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_scale_comparison.json > balance.json` for the complete, traceable quantities. The summary preserves named Resource and Service shortages, capacity bounds, assumptions, and the detail-section names; the full report retains all Definition-derived coefficients. This runs **no canonical day, Player Command, auto-procurement or gameplay optimizer**. It reads the currently registered Content Definitions and outputs nominal quantities, not claims of executable progress.

A plan has `initial_stock` (node/resource/amount_t), `initial_facilities` (node/facility/units plus optional process/extraction/duty), and a sequence of `phases` (name, days, complete set of facilities in that phase). If the Facility count rises, the difference is counted as a new construction investment at phase start, with the construction-work demand reported independently; construction does **not** wait for the capacity to finish. Removing Facilities is rejected because no retirement, salvage and dismantling model is applied. Initial Facility counts are owned historical assets and do not incur a new construction cost. All subsequent phase counts are explicit investment assumptions, not an automatic plan.

An asset can select one compatible `process` ID, with `duty` in `[0, 1]`. Its daily Process inputs/outputs are multiplied by the registered Facility throughput, units and duty. For population demand, a Facility block may specify `supported_person_days_per_day` (total assigned to that block, not per unit), limited by the installed Life Support nominal rate. Food/water/oxygen input demands come directly from the Facility LifeSupportSpec. This does not assign or move actual Population and duty-reduced Life Support shortages remain visible.

A selected `extraction` method reports installed nominal capacity separately from output. A phase may select `extraction_sites` with `node`, actual World `developed_cells` identifiers, optional authored `knowledge` rows (`cell`, `resource`, `level` from 0 to 3), and optional `day`. World geology, terrain, physical Site conditions, compatible extraction Methods, and the Extraction Domain's shared diminishing-opportunity response then produce a **conditional** Resource quantity: Cell ownership, actual Survey State, Technology, delivered power, and finite Service allocation are not validated. Opportunity is shared across eligible methods rather than counted per Facility. A context rejects an orbital World node or Cells from different bodies. Without a World context, a selected asset may use `extraction_yield_fraction` in `[0, 1]` only as an **explicit analyst assumption**, not as a derived geology/Knowledge result; without either input it credits zero planned extraction and marks opportunity unassessed. Never combine authored yield and World context for the same asset. Solar generators require `solar_yield` by node, the authored ratio of actual solar flux times availability to their reference flux, which may exceed 1. Without it the scenario fails instead of inventing power.

Phases may declare `external_rates` (node/resource/`net_t_per_day`, signed), `transfers` (origin/destination/resource/`t_per_day`), `vehicle_builds` (node/vehicle/units), and `research_stages` (node/research/stage ID), and `research_provider_allocations` (node/provider/units, exactly one of source_facility or source_vehicle, optional level/duty). These are assumed quantities, not Market orders, Fleet trips or completed Research Stages. Transfers conserve named Resource mass across sites but are only authored **delivered-volume assumptions**. `transfer_requirements` reports per-Resource directional OD demands. Optional phase `transport_allocations` rows (`origin`, `destination`, `vehicle`, `units`, optional `duty` and World `day`) reserve held Fleet units once across routes and use Transport Domain's read-only service preview for paths, cycle, direction-specific payload capacity, route blockers, resource inputs, and latency. Their upper bounds are compared against each OD's **aggregated** Resource demand. Unresolved infrastructure, Site, route, finite allocation, and Cargo settlement remain explicit even when a nominal capacity is known. A Fleet cannot simultaneously receive custom turnaround frequency and an OD service allocation. A blocked preview is not evidence that a route operates; a selected service does not settle cargo or authorize delivery. Vehicle and Research rows consume their Definition costs, and required work/RP are reported, but progression is not scheduled. An RP provider must be compatible with and not exceed the number of installed source Facilities in the same node. Fleet-backed Research Providers require compatible Vehicle capability and explicitly held Fleet; the same units cannot also be committed to Transport, Survey, Founding or Scientific Exploration. Authored Fleet turnarounds cannot overlap Research commitments. Neither type of Research Provider implies technology, Site, Power, Crew, or real finite-Service eligibility. Optional initial organization RP may be supplied as `initial_research_points` at plan level; generated RP, Stage demand, nominal pool capacity, RP shortages and Crew demand are reported separately. Research technology, Staff and provider eligibility are not verified. Typed Stage execution Service requirements are multiplied by their registered progress quantity (Theory RP, Prototype/Demonstration work); `research_service_constraints` reports the shared Organization Research execution workload and a conditional capacity-based lower bound, separately from RP generation and storage. Other Service pools and Site requirements remain explicitly unassessed. Stage execution Resource requirements are included as physical demand distinct from reserved one-time prototype materials.

`resources` keeps production, Process inputs, Maintenance, Construction, Vehicle, Research material, external transfers and shortages in distinct columns **per Resource and node**. `resource_contributions` records each Resource inflow or demand from the same quantity accounting, keyed by node/Resource/dimension/source Definition, with its Definition/World or explicit external-assumption basis. It supports tracing a shortage to competing registered Process, extraction, asset investment, ongoing maintenance, research, Fleet, spatial activity and external flow rather than treating the assumed Facility count as a correct target. Its amounts reconcile exactly to the Resource columns; it does not claim any flow was settled. Deficits are signed notional stock; negative stock is *not* legal Game State and its propagation across phases is only unmet planned balance. Power, construction, cargo handling, vehicle-production Service and special physical storage are **nominal** quantity comparisons. Generic `service_constraints` reports each required vs supplied Service rate without performing allocation. The model does not reproduce Technology/Site/Knowledge eligibility, Activity Priority, usable versus physical Storage, actual Market funds, Population transfers, automatic allocation, or actual Research Point settlement. Under explicit `extraction_sites`, physical opportunity and diminishing extraction are evaluated but not live extraction execution. Construction investment is recorded in full: `investment_constraints` compares required work against **pre-investment** installed construction capacity, giving an optimistic lower bound only when that capacity exists. Newly acquired Facilities/Fleet appear in their authored phase's nominal operation even though Construction/Assembly completion is not scheduled. `research_stages` lists typed direct prerequisites, Resource and Service requirements, work, Points and Operational Experience separately. RP generation, storage and execution capacities yield independent bounds rather than an invented combined finish day. These omissions prevent treating any result as a gameplay feasibility verdict.

An input with top-level `cases` (each `{name, plan}` or shared `{base_plan, cases: [{name, updates}]}`) also returns `differences`, computed against the first case for matching phase names and durations. Different time bases are reported as `incomparable_phase_basis`; transitions between measured and unassessed quantities are surfaced as `incomparable_unassessed_metrics`, not zero-valued gains. Values are separated by physical dimension. Do not aggregate unlike Resources or compare unmatched time windows as equivalent. Independent `evaluation_tests/test_analysis_balance.py` verifies the arithmetic and conservation of these projections; live Domain/Game contracts remain in `tests/`.

## Many Facility and Fleet assets

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_fleet_scale_comparison.json > balance.json` evaluates authored groups at several nodes and two materially different scales. This compares whether **the same economy is subjected to larger production, acquisition and upkeep demands**, not which build order is optimal. A Case may use `base_plan` at the comparison root and supply `updates` instead of copying the entire plan. An update identifies an existing row by `phase`, `collection` (`facilities`, `fleet`, or `research_provider_allocations`), `node` and `id` (Facility/Vehicle/Provider definition ID); it changes only explicitly supported fields such as `units`, `duty`, Fleet `turnarounds_per_unit_per_day`, or Provider `level`. Additional collection types are `phase` (`phase`, `days`), `solar_yield` (`node`, `factor`), and `initial_stock` (`node`, Resource `id`, `amount_t`). These edit declared input conditions only; they never introduce new records. No new Facility is created, selected, scheduled, funded, or relocated by an update. Each case inherits the same original plan independently; missing or ambiguous rows and duplicate changes are rejected. The first case is a dimensional comparison reference, not a recommended plan.

`initial_fleet` supplies historical holdings as `node`, `vehicle`, `units`, analogous to `initial_facilities`. A phase may state the **complete** Fleet holding via `fleet`, with optional `turnarounds_per_unit_per_day`, or use `vehicle_builds` to add explicit new units to prior holdings. A phase must not use both ways to specify new construction. Positive count differences consume the registered Vehicle production resources and manufacturing Service/work; existing units do not consume a second production investment. Turnaround frequency is an explicitly authored assumption: registered Vehicle turnaround resources and Service times are multiplied by that count, but the analyst does **not** manufacture a route, payload, propellant amount, Crew occupancy or free Fleet service. Removal/retirement/relocation must be modeled separately and is rejected by the holdings projection. Vehicle manufacturing completion, like Facility construction, is **not scheduled**: new units count in that phase's holdings while their full acquisition obligations are reported. This flags unscheduled construction as a limitation, not proof of immediate deployment.

Each phase additionally reports `fleet` (by Node/Vehicle, with build count and assumed turnaround cycles), `scale` (Facility and Fleet installed/new units), `system_resources` (each Resource aggregated over all nodes, including the *sum of node shortfalls*), and `system_services` (each Service aggregated over nodes, including *sum of local gaps*). Cross-node excess inventory or nominal service cannot automatically satisfy a deficient node. These totals preserve physical dimensions and **are not a composite balance score**, a gameplay feasibility verdict or an optimization target.

Typed Content variants can register a Facility or Vehicle trial in one `content_variant` list entry using `{"operation":"add", "kind":"asset_bundle", "asset_kind":"facility"|"vehicle", "id":"...", "source_id":"...", "edits":[...]}`. A Facility trial inherits its registered Construction, Decommission, Power, Storage, Construction Provider and Upgrade definitions when present. A Vehicle trial inherits the owned production and turnaround specs. Nested `edits` modify that new asset's typed owner Definitions (`facility`/`vehicle`, `construction`, `power`, etc.) before ordinary Composition validation. This is explicitly **source-derived variant authoring**, not an arbitrary new asset schema or permission to bypass physical Site/Technology validation, and it does not yet consolidate the standard Content source files. Full original standard Content migration requires a separate validated Composition refactor.

## Research / manufacturing / construction / Fleet demand across phases

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/cross_domain_industrial_research_logistics.json > balance.json`

This compares an older machine-shop portfolio, a measured-precision machining and in-line-inspected electronics portfolio, and a research/Fleet-intensive expansion. All cases request the same two phase lengths and the same basic downstream industrial processes, Fleet acquisitions, inter-node deliveries, and Research Stage point demands; the science-intensive case explicitly adds labs, housing demand and more Fleet. The science/Fleet-intensive portfolio also includes research-gated automated construction and Vehicle assembly, articulated surface freight vehicles, and their finite servicing/spare-parts load. The analysis includes real Content recipes for construction materials (including Construction Equipment), process inputs/outputs, power, annual maintenance, Fleet acquisition/turnaround materials, nominal Cargo/Construction/Vehicle Assembly/Surface Vehicle Servicing capacities, Research Providers, and Research Point storage. Any initial stocks, extraction yields, Research completion, transfers or power availability are declared assumptions, **not** verified gameplay prerequisites, self-funding plans or automatically constructed facilities.

Read *both* phase investment and continued-operation gaps. A positive nominal output does not settle a capacity shortage; a Resource held at another node cannot automatically cover a local shortage. A large simultaneous expansion is intentionally permitted to expose Construction and Fleet Assembly capacity gaps rather than inventing construction schedules or a fixed build-order recommendation. The exercise compares ways of supplying a multi-domain load and must not be treated as an optimal strategy, a calibrated victory time or a regression contract fixing Content ratios.

## World opportunity, research capability, and Fleet investment sensitivity

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/world_growth_research_logistics.json --summary`

This shared-base comparison retains an operating Earth mineral-to-ceramics process, Construction providers, Laboratory RP generation, existing Fleet, and a conditional Earth-to-orbit cargo requirement. It changes **one physical or industrial choice at a time**: developed World Cells with authored measured Knowledge, installed extraction capacity, Research Provider/Facility capacity, or acquired Fleet/selected OD service. The stage-1 theory demand is a registered Technology Stage, not a target date. The original endowment, duty and Fleet assignments are disclosed in the JSON as scenario conditions; they are not recommended construction totals or the game's actual starting state.

The World changes use registered Earth Cell potential and Extraction Domain's shared diminishing response. An extra developed Cell and extra mining rigs therefore change different physical quantities, with their additional capital/maintenance effects remaining visible. The comparison deliberately does **not** create Operational Nodes, Survey completions, Research Stage progress or Fleet routes. A missing Transport path is reported as a blocker, and nominal capacity does not satisfy the attributed cargo transfer. Use the full JSON report when tracing the Resource and Service causes of a difference; no case is automatically a winner.

## Spatial expansion, Founding, Survey, Scientific Exploration, and Market boundaries

These opt-in activity rows all use the same `phases` input and the same Resource/Service accounting; no additional simulation loop exists.

- `spatial_projects`: `{node, recipe, units}` uses the registered Spatial Development Recipe's one-time Resource and construction-work obligations. It does not add developed Cells; selected `extraction_sites` remain separately declared hypothetical conditions.
- `upgrade_projects`: `{node, facility, target_level, units}` records registered Facility Upgrade material and Construction work for installed units, not immediate Level changes. It does not modify operational throughput, research provider levels or upgrade sequencing; those must be assessed as separate chosen configurations with unresolved Level eligibility.
- `founding_projects`: `{staging_node, recipe, target_cell|target_node, vehicle?}` debits the registered deployment payload once at the staging Node and includes its preparation Service demand. The deployed Facilities and initial inventory are **potential payload destinations**, not credited to an invented new Operational Node. A selected Vehicle reserves the recipe-required Fleet units from the same finite holdings used by Transport, Scientific Exploration, and Survey.
- `science_campaigns`: `{campaign, vehicle?}` contributes the registered consumable Resources and potential total Research Point reward/duration, not earned RP. An optional Vehicle similarly reserves required Fleet units; unassessed mission/technology/movement/crew provision eligibility remains explicit. Reusing a campaign across phases is rejected rather than multiplying its reward.
- `survey_activities`: `{node, provider, source, mode, units, cell, resource, starting_progress?}` compares the registered Survey Target threshold and compatible Provider/Mode capacity. `starting_progress` is an **analyst-provided** point value; omitted progress yields an unassessed time bound, never an assumed zero. Source units are reserved against the selected Facility or Fleet holdings. Reach, survey knowledge, power, site and actual allocation are not inferred.
- `market_buys`: `{node, provider, resource, requested_t}` draws the registered Market Provider's buy price, supply stock/replenishment, and lead time into provider-wide conditional upper bounds. Requests by multiple Nodes share one provider's supply. No Market order, provider State, funds debit, or Resource inflow is settled. Optional phase `assumed_funds_musd` compares an explicit funding envelope with nominal order cost; it is not an organization Funds State. Ordinary `external_rates` remain an explicitly unrestricted analyst settlement assumption, not evidence that such a Market purchase is possible.

Registered dependencies and their quantitative units, conditions, and provenance are attached as `graph_evidence` to selected activities through the existing Definition Graph. A graph relation is **explanatory**, never automatically an executed Resource/Service flow. The compact summary retains the constraints and points to full quantitative sections. Zero available construction capacity yields an unassessed investment-duration bound, not a finite project completion date.
