# Quantity balance comparisons (developer-only)

Use `python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_scale_comparison.json --summary` for a compact overview, then `python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_scale_comparison.json > balance.json` for the complete, traceable quantities. The summary preserves named Resource and Service shortages, capacity bounds, assumptions, and the detail-section names. Multi-case summaries show the complete baseline once and explicit changed quantities only for alternatives (`relative_to_baseline`, `changed_phases`); an omitted alternative quantity is unchanged, never zero or automatically feasible. Individual shortages show only up to three leading activity drivers and their total count; full ledgers and all case differences remain in the unabridged report. This runs **no canonical day, Player Command, auto-procurement or gameplay optimizer**. It reads the currently registered Content Definitions and outputs nominal quantities, not claims of executable progress.

Case differences can also use `{"action":"add", "collection":"facilities"|"fleet"|"research_provider_allocations", "phase":"...", "row":{...}}` to select an additional registered asset, compatible provider assignment, one-shot passenger requirement or registered science campaign without copying the full phase. The same definition-backed acquisition, operation, maintenance, RP and Fleet-sharing calculations apply as in a full plan. Additions must name a phase, node (or passenger origin/destination) and Definition explicitly; they do not create a source automatically, cannot duplicate an existing source or activity, and do not create/settle authoritative State. Phase-to-phase holdings remain complete selected compositions: when a newly chosen Facility or Fleet is not carried into a later phase, the ordinary removal prohibition still applies. This is an input-composition sensitivity, not an optimizer or automatic construction schedule.

A plan has `initial_stock` (node/resource/amount_t), `initial_facilities` (node/facility/units plus optional process/extraction/duty), and a sequence of `phases` (name, days, complete set of facilities in that phase). If the Facility count rises, the difference is counted as a new construction investment at phase start, with the construction-work demand reported independently; construction does **not** wait for the capacity to finish. Removing Facilities is rejected because no retirement, salvage and dismantling model is applied. Initial Facility counts are owned historical assets and do not incur a new construction cost. All subsequent phase counts are explicit investment assumptions, not an automatic plan.

An asset can select one compatible `process` ID, with `duty` in `[0, 1]`. Its daily Process inputs/outputs are multiplied by the registered Facility throughput, units and duty. For population demand, a Facility block may specify `supported_person_days_per_day` (total assigned to that block, not per unit), limited by the installed Life Support nominal rate. Food/water/oxygen input demands come directly from the Facility LifeSupportSpec. `housing_nominal_people` counts physical installed places and compares them to the explicitly assigned person-days/day as a conditional resident count. Neither housing nor Life Support proves Population admission, actual occupancy, Crew allocation or a passenger transfer; duty-reduced Life Support shortages remain visible.

A selected `extraction` method reports installed nominal capacity separately from output. A phase may select `extraction_sites` with `node`, actual World `developed_cells` identifiers, optional authored `knowledge` rows (`cell`, `resource`, `level` from 0 to 3), and optional `day`. World geology, terrain, physical Site conditions, compatible extraction Methods, and the Extraction Domain's shared diminishing-opportunity response then produce a **conditional** Resource quantity: Cell ownership, actual Survey State, Technology, delivered power, and finite Service allocation are not validated. Opportunity is shared across eligible methods rather than counted per Facility. A context rejects an orbital World node or Cells from different bodies. Without a World context, a selected asset may use `extraction_yield_fraction` in `[0, 1]` only as an **explicit analyst assumption**, not as a derived geology/Knowledge result; without either input it credits zero planned extraction and marks opportunity unassessed. Never combine authored yield and World context for the same asset. Solar generators require an explicit Power condition. `solar_yield` is an authored by-node ratio of actual solar flux times availability to reference flux, which may exceed 1. Alternatively, top-level `solar_sites` rows (`node`, `facility`, `context`, optional `day`) use a registered World Surface Cell or non-surface Spatial Node and the **Power Domain's** nominal environmental generation calculation for that specific solar Facility. That site's illumination is a selected World condition, **not** established Facility placement, solar availability over an entire multi-day phase, delivered/allocated Power, or known Construction/Knowledge eligibility. The authored yield and a selected World solar site may not overlap for one Node; a missing solar condition is rejected rather than silently inventing power.

Phases may declare `external_rates` (node/resource/`net_t_per_day`, signed), `transfers` (origin/destination/resource/`t_per_day`), `vehicle_builds` (node/vehicle/units), and `research_stages` (node/research/stage ID), and `research_provider_allocations` (node/provider/units, exactly one of source_facility or source_vehicle, optional level/duty). These are assumed quantities, not Market orders, Fleet trips or completed Research Stages. Transfers conserve named Resource mass across sites but are only authored **delivered-volume assumptions**. `received_t` is conditional, not a guaranteed usable receipt; the same ledger reports cumulative unverified Cargo, `notional_end_without_assumed_arrivals_t`, and `shortfall_without_assumed_arrivals_t` as a conservative no-arrivals comparison across phases. The compact summary lists `conditional_incoming_cargo_risks_by_node` even when optimistic conditional inflows conceal a shortage. This does not rerun Logistics or schedule arrival. `transfer_requirements` reports per-Resource directional OD demands. Optional phase `transport_allocations` rows (`origin`, `destination`, `vehicle`, `units`, optional `duty` and World `day`) reserve held Fleet units once across routes and use Transport Domain's read-only service preview for paths, cycle, direction-specific payload capacity, route blockers, resource inputs, and latency. Their upper bounds are compared against each OD's **aggregated** Resource demand. Unresolved infrastructure, Site, route, finite allocation, and Cargo settlement remain explicit even when a nominal capacity is known. A Fleet cannot simultaneously receive custom turnaround frequency and an OD service allocation. A blocked preview is not evidence that a route operates; a selected service does not settle cargo or authorize delivery. Vehicle and Research rows consume their Definition costs, and required work/RP are reported, but progression is not scheduled. An RP provider must be compatible with and not exceed the number of installed source Facilities in the same node. Fleet-backed Research Providers require compatible Vehicle capability and explicitly held Fleet; the same units cannot also be committed to Transport, Survey, Founding or Scientific Exploration. Authored Fleet turnarounds cannot overlap Research commitments. Neither type of Research Provider implies technology, Site, Power, Crew, or real finite-Service eligibility. Optional initial organization RP may be supplied as `initial_research_points` at plan level; generated RP, Stage demand, nominal pool capacity, RP shortages and Crew demand are reported separately. Research technology, Staff and provider eligibility are not verified. Typed Stage execution Service requirements are multiplied by their registered progress quantity (Theory RP, Prototype/Demonstration work); `research_service_constraints` reports the shared Organization Research execution workload and a conditional capacity-based lower bound, separately from RP generation and storage. Other Service pools and Site requirements remain explicitly unassessed. Stage execution Resource requirements are included as physical demand distinct from reserved one-time prototype materials.

Optional phase `passenger_transfers` records **dedicated one-shot** requests (`origin`, `destination`, passenger-rated `vehicle`, `units`, `people`, and positive `assumed_onboard_days`). Each request reserves selected Fleet against Research, Transport, Survey, Founding, other Fleet turnarounds and Scientific Exploration; it does not award Population, claim completed Movement, create a passenger-only Transport network, or settle arrivals. The Transport-owned Vehicle Definition's Passenger Accommodation supplies actual seat/life-support/onboard-Power limits, standard passenger mass and net Food/Water/Oxygen per person-day via its existing pure calculation contract. Named Resource requirements are one-time **requested embarkation provisions**, not a simulated departure or daily Resource consumption in the origin Node. A vehicle's nameplate payload provides only a generous upper bound; path-specific delta-v/propellant and real Cargo mass allocation are unassessed. A seat or nameplate mass shortfall stays visible instead of inventing extra trips or Fleet. The comparison never credits destination residents/Housing or Life Support, and it never treats a chosen onboard duration as resolved latency or proof of mission endurance. Scheduled Service passenger dispatches share Cargo/Fleet mass and are **not** computed by this dedicated-trip input; they remain under the actual Population/Transport/Logistics owner contracts.

`resources` keeps production, Process inputs, Maintenance, Construction, Vehicle, Research material, external transfers and shortages in distinct columns **per Resource and node**. `one_time_investment_and_activity_demand_t` separates facility/vehicle acquisition and selected one-shot Research, Scientific Exploration and Founding requirements from Process, Extraction, Maintenance, Fleet operation, Life Support and recurring OD activity. `nominal_recurring_net_t_per_day` is the assumed operating rate rather than total phase net divided by days. `conditional_stock_exhaustion_days_after_investment` is a stationary-rate sensitivity reference only when opening notional Stock covers the one-time burden and the recurring net rate is negative; it is **not** a depletion forecast, delivery promise or proof that assets are built. `opening_investment_stock_shortfall_t` exposes immediate gaps separately from ongoing shortages. The compact summary retains shortage-origin drivers and nominal power, Housing, Life Support, Resource and Service pressures without ranking strategies.  `resource_contributions` records each Resource inflow or demand from the same quantity accounting, keyed by node/Resource/dimension/source Definition, with its Definition/World or explicit external-assumption basis. It supports tracing a shortage to competing registered Process, extraction, asset investment, ongoing maintenance, research, Fleet, spatial activity and external flow rather than treating the assumed Facility count as a correct target. Its amounts reconcile exactly to the Resource columns; it does not claim any flow was settled. `service_contributions` uses the same ledger principle for node-scoped Service demand and nameplate supply, including Construction work normalized per comparison day, Vehicle assembly, Fleet operations, Transport, Research execution at selected node scope, Founding preparation and Cargo handling. Shared Organization Research execution remains a separate `research_service_constraints` dimension; neither ledger performs allocation or proves availability. Deficits are signed notional stock; negative stock is *not* legal Game State and its propagation across phases is only unmet planned balance. Power, construction, cargo handling, vehicle-production Service and special physical storage are **nominal** quantity comparisons. Generic `service_constraints` reports each required vs supplied Service rate without performing allocation. The model does not reproduce Technology/Site/Knowledge eligibility, Activity Priority, usable versus physical Storage, actual Market funds, Population transfers, automatic allocation, or actual Research Point settlement. Under explicit `extraction_sites`, physical opportunity and diminishing extraction are evaluated but not live extraction execution. Construction investment is recorded in full: `investment_constraints` compares required work against **pre-investment** installed construction capacity, giving an optimistic lower bound only when that capacity exists. Newly acquired Facilities/Fleet appear in their authored phase's nominal operation even though Construction/Assembly completion is not scheduled. `research_stages` lists typed direct prerequisites, Resource and Service requirements, work, Points and Operational Experience separately. RP generation, storage and execution capacities yield independent bounds rather than an invented combined finish day. These omissions prevent treating any result as a gameplay feasibility verdict.

A `facilities` case update may change the selected compatible `process` or `extraction` Method on one existing operating Facility row without changing its Definition or silently selecting an optimal Recipe. It may also vary assigned Life Support person-days/day and explicitly authored extraction yield where that assumption is used. The original Process/Extraction kind must be preserved, and validation still uses the same registered Definitions. Changing an input method never changes Technology/Knowledge eligibility or the installed Facility's physical capability.

An input with top-level `cases` (each `{name, plan}` or shared `{base_plan, cases: [{name, updates}]}`) also returns `differences`, computed against the first case for matching phase names and durations. Different time bases are reported as `incomparable_phase_basis`; transitions between measured and unassessed quantities are surfaced as `incomparable_unassessed_metrics`, not zero-valued gains. Values are separated by physical dimension. Do not aggregate unlike Resources or compare unmatched time windows as equivalent. Independent `evaluation_tests/test_analysis_balance.py` verifies the arithmetic and conservation of these projections; live Domain/Game contracts remain in `tests/`.

## Many Facility and Fleet assets

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/industrial_fleet_scale_comparison.json > balance.json` evaluates authored groups at several nodes and two materially different scales. This compares whether **the same economy is subjected to larger production, acquisition and upkeep demands**, not which build order is optimal. A Case may use `base_plan` at the comparison root and supply `updates` instead of copying the entire plan. An update identifies an existing row by `phase`, `collection` (`facilities`, `fleet`, or `research_provider_allocations`), `node` and `id` (Facility/Vehicle/Provider definition ID); it changes only explicitly supported fields such as `units`, `duty`, Fleet `turnarounds_per_unit_per_day`, or Provider `level`. Additional collection types are `phase` (`phase`, `days`), `solar_yield` (`node`, `factor`), `solar_sites` (`node`, `id` = solar Facility, `context` or `day`), and `initial_stock` (`node`, Resource `id`, `amount_t`). These edit declared input conditions only; they never introduce new records. No new Facility is created, selected, scheduled, funded, or relocated by an update. Each case inherits the same original plan independently; missing or ambiguous rows and duplicate changes are rejected. The first case is a dimensional comparison reference, not a recommended plan.

`initial_fleet` supplies historical holdings as `node`, `vehicle`, `units`, analogous to `initial_facilities`. A phase may state the **complete** Fleet holding via `fleet`, with optional `turnarounds_per_unit_per_day`, or use `vehicle_builds` to add explicit new units to prior holdings. A phase must not use both ways to specify new construction. Positive count differences consume the registered Vehicle production resources and manufacturing Service/work; existing units do not consume a second production investment. Turnaround frequency is an explicitly authored assumption: registered Vehicle turnaround resources and Service times are multiplied by that count, but the analyst does **not** manufacture a route, payload, propellant amount, Crew occupancy or free Fleet service. Removal/retirement/relocation must be modeled separately and is rejected by the holdings projection. Vehicle manufacturing completion, like Facility construction, is **not scheduled**: new units count in that phase's holdings while their full acquisition obligations are reported. This flags unscheduled construction as a limitation, not proof of immediate deployment.

Each phase additionally reports `fleet` (by Node/Vehicle, with build count and assumed turnaround cycles), `scale` (Facility and Fleet installed/new units), `system_resources` (each Resource aggregated over all nodes, including the *sum of node shortfalls*), and `system_services` (each Service aggregated over nodes, including *sum of local gaps*). Cross-node excess inventory or nominal service cannot automatically satisfy a deficient node. These totals preserve physical dimensions and **are not a composite balance score**, a gameplay feasibility verdict or an optimization target.

Typed Content variants can register a Facility or Vehicle trial in one `content_variant` list entry using `{"operation":"add", "kind":"asset_bundle", "asset_kind":"facility"|"vehicle", "id":"...", "source_id":"...", "edits":[...]}`. A Facility trial inherits its registered Construction, Decommission, Power, Storage, Construction Provider and Upgrade definitions when present. A Vehicle trial inherits the owned production and turnaround specs. Nested `edits` modify that new asset's typed owner Definitions (`facility`/`vehicle`, `construction`, `power`, etc.) before ordinary Composition validation. This is explicitly **source-derived variant authoring**, not an arbitrary new asset schema or permission to bypass physical Site/Technology validation, and it does not yet consolidate the standard Content source files. Full original standard Content migration requires a separate validated Composition refactor.

## Research / manufacturing / construction / Fleet demand across phases

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/cross_domain_industrial_research_logistics.json > balance.json`

This compares an older machine-shop portfolio, a measured-precision machining and in-line-inspected electronics portfolio, and a research/Fleet-intensive expansion. All cases request the same two phase lengths and the same basic downstream industrial processes, Fleet acquisitions, inter-node deliveries, and Research Stage point demands; the science-intensive case explicitly adds labs, housing demand and more Fleet. The science/Fleet-intensive portfolio also includes research-gated automated construction and Vehicle assembly, articulated surface freight vehicles, and their finite servicing/spare-parts load. The analysis includes real Content recipes for construction materials (including Construction Equipment), process inputs/outputs, power, annual maintenance, Fleet acquisition/turnaround materials, nominal Cargo/Construction/Vehicle Assembly/Surface Vehicle Servicing capacities, Research Providers, and Research Point storage. Any initial stocks, extraction yields, Research completion, transfers or power availability are declared assumptions, **not** verified gameplay prerequisites, self-funding plans or automatically constructed facilities.

Read *both* phase investment and continued-operation gaps. A positive nominal output does not settle a capacity shortage; a Resource held at another node cannot automatically cover a local shortage. A large simultaneous expansion is intentionally permitted to expose Construction and Fleet Assembly capacity gaps rather than inventing construction schedules or a fixed build-order recommendation. The exercise compares ways of supplying a multi-domain load and must not be treated as an optimal strategy, a calibrated victory time or a regression contract fixing Content ratios.

This shared-base comparison retains an operating Earth mineral-to-ceramics process, Construction providers, Laboratory RP generation, existing Fleet, and a conditional Earth-to-orbit cargo requirement. It changes **one physical or industrial choice at a time**: developed World Cells with authored measured Knowledge, installed extraction capacity, Research Provider/Facility capacity, or acquired Fleet/selected OD service. The stage-1 theory demand is a registered Technology Stage, not a target date. ## Spatial expansion, Founding, Survey, Scientific Exploration, and Market boundaries

These opt-in activity rows all use the same `phases` input and the same Resource/Service accounting; no additional simulation loop exists.

- `spatial_projects`: `{node, recipe, units}` uses the registered Spatial Development Recipe's one-time Resource and construction-work obligations. It does not add developed Cells; selected `extraction_sites` remain separately declared hypothetical conditions.
- `upgrade_projects`: `{node, facility, target_level, units}` records registered Facility Upgrade material and Construction work for installed units, not immediate Level changes. Its additional post-completion maintenance is a separate conditional per-Resource/day quantity computed by the Facility Domain, not a booked phase flow before construction. Compatible Research Provider levels also expose conditional before/after RP generation, execution and storage capacities; those are not credited to the investment phase. It does not modify operational throughput, research provider levels or upgrade sequencing; those must be assessed as separate chosen configurations with unresolved Level eligibility.
- `founding_projects`: `{staging_node, recipe, target_cell|target_node, vehicle?}` debits the registered deployment payload once at the staging Node and includes its preparation Service demand. The deployed Facilities and initial inventory are **potential payload destinations**, not credited to an invented new Operational Node. A selected Vehicle reserves the recipe-required Fleet units from the same finite holdings used by Transport, Scientific Exploration, and Survey.
- `science_campaigns`: `{campaign, vehicle?}` contributes the registered consumable Resources and potential total Research Point reward/duration, not earned RP. An optional Vehicle similarly reserves required Fleet units; unassessed mission/technology/movement/crew provision eligibility remains explicit. Reusing a campaign across phases is rejected rather than multiplying its reward.
- `survey_activities`: `{node, provider, source, mode, units, cell, resource, starting_progress?}` compares the registered Survey Target threshold and compatible Provider/Mode capacity. `starting_progress` is an **analyst-provided** point value; omitted progress yields an unassessed time bound, never an assumed zero. Source units are reserved against the selected Facility or Fleet holdings. Reach, survey knowledge, power, site and actual allocation are not inferred.
- `market_buys`: `{node, provider, resource, requested_t}` draws the registered Market Provider's buy price, supply stock/replenishment, and lead time into provider-wide conditional upper bounds. Requests by multiple Nodes share one provider's supply. No Market order, provider State, funds debit, or Resource inflow is settled. Optional phase `assumed_funds_musd` compares an explicit funding envelope with nominal order cost; it is not an organization Funds State. Ordinary `external_rates` remain an explicitly unrestricted analyst settlement assumption, not evidence that such a Market purchase is possible.

Registered dependencies and their quantitative units, conditions, and provenance are attached as `graph_evidence` to selected activities through the existing Definition Graph. A graph relation is **explanatory**, never automatically an executed Resource/Service flow. The compact summary retains the constraints and points to full quantitative sections. Zero available construction capacity yields an unassessed investment-duration bound, not a finite project completion date.

## Cross-domain industrial, research, exploration, founding and settlement trade-offs

`python scripts/analysis_balance.py evaluation_plans/connected_industry_science_settlement_tradeoffs.json --summary`

This shared-base analysis connects conditional terrestrial mineral/ore/water extraction, ore recovery and fabrication, Earth research and assembly, an Earth–orbit–lunar-orbit freight request, Fleet investment, remote Survey, scientific exploration, one-time surface Founding, Market limits, and a **separately assumed** later lunar operation with volatile recovery, power, construction, Life Support and Housing. The first phase does not award Founding or Science results, and the second phase's prospective surface composition is **not** treated as a completed deployment. Planetary conditions and Knowledge are explicit World/analyst inputs, not imported live Survey State. Earth, orbit and a not-yet-founded surface Node retain separate inventories; no outbound material is silently delivered to the surface.

The twenty-three comparisons vary operating ore-processing chemistry, mining capacity versus developed Resource Opportunity, high-illumination surface power siting versus cold-trap volatile access, power versus extraction investment, research equipment capacity, supported population, and selected launch Fleet. Baseline Equipment, Fleet and initial Stocks are analyst-selected comparison inputs rather than recommended game objectives. In the current registered Content, wet ore recovery exchanges Water demand for higher Metal Feedstock output; adding mines on the existing Cell has a different throughput response from developing a new Cell. A single surface solar installation at a resource-rich cold trap remains severely Power-limited on the selected reference day, whereas a sunlit ridge has materially higher nameplate generation but requires separate Cell development and **unassessed** distribution. The lunar Process output is conditional on its Resource, finite Power and Site requirements rather than an actual recovered stock. These observations identify competing engineering burdens, not an instruction to inflate mining, Market or starting Stock to make any chosen plan succeed.

The three dedicated Earth-to-orbit passenger-request sensitivities compare six, twelve and thirteen requested people aboard the same two registered crew-rated reusable launch Vehicles. They use explicitly varied onboard days and the Vehicle's actual seat, Life Support, Power and per-person Resource coefficients. Twelve are within the two Vehicle units' nameplate seats, whereas thirteen exceed them; neither case asserts an actual route or assured passenger delivery. Unlike the conditional steady cargo request, this one-shot load consumes named embarkation provisions only in the source Node and does not create People at the destination or assume a second Fleet. This makes the trade-off between transport payload, Food/Water/Oxygen and existing Facility/Research maintenance visible without converting passengers to Resource cargo.

The crewed Mars-orbit scientific expedition alternative adds one registered nuclear-thermal crew ship to the Fleet in both phases and selects the actual registered campaign only in the preparation phase. Construction/Vehicle assembly costs, specialist shielding and thermal control inputs, finite manufacturing Service, and the campaign's own one-time consumables enter the existing ledger. For an unfounded physical destination, the game-owned Scientific Exploration contract requires onboard provisions throughout the registered on-site scientific activity; the selected Vehicle's passenger accommodation therefore supplies a **site-duration-only lower bound** for additional Food, Water, Oxygen and crew mass. Movement flight and return time, propellant, Crew availability, destination eligibility and the potential science RP award remain **unassessed**, so the 22-day registered active scientific stage is not a full mission time or a route-capacity claim. For established destinations the on-site period is not assumed to be spent onboard; without a physical route the added passenger provision cannot be evaluated and is explicitly unknown rather than zero actual demand.

### Research asset generation, geography and investment sensitivities

The same portfolio also compares explicitly added Tier 2 microgravity laboratory (orbit), Tier 3 crewed orbital laboratory, Tier 3 surface sample-analysis laboratory (Earth), Tier 4 vacuum-regolith laboratory (prospective lunar surface) and Tier 4 materials-fatigue facility (Earth) against the same industrial, science, population and freight base. Each case selects **one** additional compatible Facility and Research Provider; resource investment, construction-work, electricity, maintenance, RP generation/storage and Organization-wide finite Research execution capacity are derived from the same registered Definitions. The new composition supplies nominal RP/Research execution immediately only as a **post-acquisition conditional operating comparison**; the reported investment burden has not been settled. Facility Placement, prerequisite Technology, actual Crew and Power, upstream component sourcing, orbital freight, site/founding eligibility and finite allocation may prevent operation.

In the current Content, the shared 3000-RP Theory requirement exposes the bottleneck transition: the baseline 3/日 Research execution capacity gives a 1000-day conditional execution lower bound; additions of the Tier 2 orbital platform, Tier 3 crewed orbital lab, Tier 3 terrestrial sample lab, Tier 4 lunar regolith lab and Tier 4 terrestrial materials lab respectively increase nominal shared capacity to 5.5, 8, 9, 15 and 27/日, giving corresponding isolated bounds of approximately 545, 375, 333, 200 and 111 days. These are **not** intended research durations, balancing targets, completed research, or guarantees of feasibility. The higher-tier cases consume markedly different construction materials, work, power, and in some cases finite Crew; the lunar regolith and terrestrial materials cases add power load to compositions that already have other energy deficits. New Research Providers do not upgrade historical assets retroactively or solve Resource/Fleet delivery constraints. This comparison exists to expose the RP-generation-versus-execution bottleneck, engineering burden and location choice without declaring a single best generation or Facility count.

Two further configurations connect Tier 4 materials research acquisition to **actual registered upstream component Process** activity at low and expanded scales. The shielding and optical assembly duty fractions are calculated from the downstream beamline acquisition recipe (4 shielding panels and 0.8 optical instrument packages per unit), the two Process output coefficients, their Facility throughputs, the selected acquisition count (1 or 10) and the 120-day comparison basis. They are *not* target self-sufficiency rates or evidence that construction may use a product before its factory is finished. Supplying nominal acquisition quantities does not cover those assets' continuing Maintenance or the two new factories' own Construction/Power/Input requirements: the two specialist Resources retain small nominal deficits even when the selected lines produce the full acquisition amount. Increasing beamline scale makes the energy/Component and upstream industrial burden visible, rather than providing a license to mint shielding or optics through a transfer. Procurement, Technology, Crew and execution priority remain unassessed.


Inspect detailed `resource_contributions`/`service_contributions`, conditional Research Stage bounds, pre-investment Construction work, transit blockers and all missing eligibility conditions before changing any Content coefficients. Current OD service previews can remain unassessed; additional Fleet does not itself prove Cargo delivery. A potential `conditional_stock_exhaustion_days_after_investment` is a steady-rate *scale* only. Do not compare unlike phase durations as if their projected Stock deltas were equivalent, or treat the phase-opening completed assets as a scheduled Construction/Founding result.
